## Integration status registry

`AVAILABLE` is used only after a successful supported operation/probe. `DEGRADED` means a partial capability or explicitly non-durable mode. `UNAVAILABLE` means a configured dependency failed its probe. `NOT_CONFIGURED` means no adapter/credentials are present. `UNVERIFIED` means configuration or a portal exists but operation/access has not been verified. The extraction service probe is a bounded `/health` request. AI, Comtrade and commercial services remain unverified until a real operation is intentionally run.

No credentials were found in this turn, and no paid or government operation was executed. Rates, costs, and access limits must be obtained from current provider contracts; none are guessed here.
